SNPio 1.7.7 is a stable bug-fix release.

### Corrected allele counting

PR [#51](https://github.com/btmartin721/SNPio/pull/51) corrects homozygote counting in MAF, MAC, and singleton filters. Each homozygous sample now contributes two allele copies; each heterozygous sample contributes one copy of each allele.

The MAF/MAC defect affects **SNPio 1.6.0–1.7.6**. Depending on the genotype composition, affected versions can underestimate MAF or overestimate MAC and retain or remove the wrong loci. Recheck filtering results produced with affected versions.
